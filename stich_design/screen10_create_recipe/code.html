Cooking Steps

Step-by-step instructions with cooking timers

1

Press & Prep Organic Tofu

Remove the tofu block from its water bath and wrap firmly in layered unbleached kitchen towels or cheesecloth. Place a heavy cast-iron pan or weighted press atop for exactly 15 minutes to express residual moisture. Slice cleanly into uniform 2cm (0.8-inch) cubes for optimal caramelization surface area.

2

Tamari Sesame Marinade Infusion

In a shallow ceramic dish, vigorously whisk the tamari, toasted sesame oil, and minced garlic until emulsified. Gently tumble the pressed cubes through the bath, coating each facet evenly. Let rest for 10 minutes at room temperature, tossing halfway through to ensure complete moisture absorption.

3

Cornstarch Crust & High-Heat Crisp

Dredge marinated cubes in organic cornstarch mixed with sesame seeds until a powdery dry crust forms. Heat a well-seasoned carbon steel skillet over medium-high heat with a high smoke-point oil. Sear each cube face for 2–3 minutes without crowding until deep golden and acoustically crispy.
