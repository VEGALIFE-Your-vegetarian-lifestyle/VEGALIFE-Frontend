You can easily hit 32g of complete plant protein using a clean, whole-food synergy instead of ultra-processed meat analogues. Here is the recommended stir-fry base:

~32g Total Protein
415 kcal Energy
11.4g Dietary Fiber
  • check_circle Extra-Firm Pressed Tofu (150g): Yields ~22g protein with high calcium content. Press for 15 mins to maximize glaze absorption.
  • check_circle Steamed Shelled Edamame (50g / ~1/3 cup): Adds ~6g protein and a complete amino acid profile (notably lysine).
  • check_circle Nutritional Yeast & Toasted Hemp Seeds Garnish (1 tbsp each): Supplies the final ~4g protein plus active B-vitamins.
