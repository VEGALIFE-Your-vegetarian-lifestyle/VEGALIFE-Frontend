About Green Sprout Bistro

Founded in 2021 in the heart of Portland's Pearl District, Green Sprout Bistro is dedicated to reimagining compassionate, mindful eating without sacrificing depth, texture, or indulgence. We believe that whole-food plant nutrition is the most vibrant way to fuel human health and heal our living biosphere.

Every single sauce, nut creamer, and grain ferment is prepared from scratch in our open kitchen. We partner exclusively with Willamette Valley organic growers, harvesting seasonal greens and wild Pacific Northwest mushrooms at their nutritional peak. No hydrogenated oils, zero synthetic preservatives, and zero animal exploitation.
