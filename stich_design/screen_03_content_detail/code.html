Crisp Perfection Without Deep Frying

Achieving that deeply satisfying, caramelized exterior on plant proteins often leads home cooks to deep frying or dousing cubes in excessive cooking oil. Through years of professional kitchen trials, I found that thorough water extraction paired with an ultra-thin cornstarch dust and convection air heat produces irresistible restaurant-caliber crunch while preserving the tender core.

Paired with earthy quinoa, lightly steamed tenderstem greens, and a nutty sesame-tamari glaze, this bowl satisfies cravings, balances complete amino acids, and delivers steady post-meal energy without lethargy.

Cooking Instructions

Follow method sequentially for maximum texture

4 Steps
1

Press & Cube Tofu

Wrap the tofu block securely in clean kitchen towels or unbleached paper towels. Set a heavy cast-iron skillet or cutting board weighted with canned goods on top for 10 minutes to expel interior moisture. Once pressed, cut into uniform 2 cm (3/4-inch) bite-sized cubes.

2

Marinate in Tamari Glaze

In a shallow mixing bowl, whisk together the tamari soy sauce, toasted sesame oil, and a dash of grated garlic if desired. Gently fold in the tofu cubes, rotating to coat every surface evenly. Allow the cubes to absorb the rich savory emulsion for 5 minutes.

3

Coat & Air-Fry at 200°C

Sprinkle the cornstarch evenly across the marinated cubes and toss lightly until a soft, translucent powdery film coats each piece. Preheat the air fryer to 200°C (400°F). Spread cubes in a single layer with space between them. Air-fry for 14 minutes, shaking the basket halfway, until blistered and golden.

4

Assemble Quinoa Bowls & Garnish

Divide fluffy warm quinoa between two artisanal wide bowls. Arrange the crispy golden tofu, steamed broccoli florets, creamy avocado slices, and ribboned cucumbers in sections. Finish with a generous sprinkle of toasted sesame seeds and fresh scallions. Serve immediately while hot!

tips_and_updates

Chef Marcus’s Kitchen Secret

For that glass-shatter crunch: Toss the tofu in cornstarch right before dropping into the heated air fryer basket. Don’t let cornstarch sit on wet tofu for too long before frying, or it will turn pasty instead of crisp!
