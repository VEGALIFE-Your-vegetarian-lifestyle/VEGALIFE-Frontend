Crispy Golden Sesame Crusted Tofu

Dish Name: Tofu Bowl

Ultra-crispy outside with tender seasoned protein within. Marinated with naturally fermented tamari and aromatic toasted sesame oil, then seared until golden.

Ingredients

4 documented items
1. Organic Firm Tofu 400 grams
2. Toasted Sesame Oil 1.5 tbsp
3. Low-Sodium Tamari 2 tbsp
4. Cornstarch 1.25 tbsp

Preparation & Cooking Instructions

Step 1: Press the block of firm tofu for 15 minutes with a clean kitchen towel to expel excess moisture, then cut into uniform 1-inch cubes.

Step 2: In a shallow bowl, whisk tamari and sesame oil. Gently coat tofu cubes, letting them absorb marinade for 5 minutes before tossing lightly in cornstarch.

Step 3: Heat non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Sear tofu cubes 3–4 minutes per side until a crisp golden sesame crust forms. Serve immediately over fresh greens or warm quinoa.
