Golden Maple Sesame Tempeh Bowl

Charred tempeh glazed in maple and toasted sesame, over warm brown rice with quick-pickled cabbage. Sweet, savoury, and ready in half an hour.

Published
Prep
10 min
Cook
15 min
Servings
2
Difficulty
Medium

Ingredients 5

  • 01tempeh200 g
  • 02maple syrup2 tbsp
  • 03toasted sesame oil1 tsp
  • 04brown rice1 cup
  • 05red cabbage120 g

Cooking Steps 3

  1. 1

    Bloom the aromatics

    Warm the sesame oil over medium heat and add the grated ginger and garlic. Stir until fragrant, about 40 seconds.

  2. 2

    Steam the tempeh

    Steam the tempeh slabs for 10 minutes to mellow any bitterness, then pat dry and cube.

  3. 3

    Roast until golden

    Toss with maple and sesame, roast at 200°C for 15 minutes until the edges caramelise.
